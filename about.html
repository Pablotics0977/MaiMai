About You

You are one of the most beautiful people I know, not just because of how you look, but because of the person you are. Your smile somehow makes my day better, and your laugh is honestly one of my favorite sounds.

Being with you makes even the simplest moments feel special. Kahit ordinary day lang, you somehow have a way of making it feel a little brighter just by being there.

I created this website as a little reminder of how special you are to me and how much I appreciate having you in my life. Gusto kong may something ka na pwede mong balikan whenever you want, just to remind you kung gaano ka ka-special sa’kin.

I also love all the little things that make you who you are—yung mga random thoughts mo, pang-aasar, pagiging makulit, biglang pag-send ng kung anu-anong reels, mga tanong mong minsan napapaisip talaga ako kung saan mo nakuha HAHAHA, at yung mga simpleng moments na ikaw lang talaga ang makakagawa. Even your little tampo and pagiging mataray sometimes somehow became part of the things I’ve grown to appreciate about you.

Your kindness, your strength, and even the little things you do without realizing it inspire me in ways you probably don't know. You make me want to become a better version of myself, and I'm really grateful for that.

Thank you for being you for your patience, your understanding, your little quirks, your kakulitan, and for choosing to let me be part of your life. I'm really glad I met you, and even more glad na I get to know you a little more every day.

You mean more to me than you probably realize❤️
