A Letter For You

My Dearest Maimai,

I honestly don't know where to start, kasi ang funny isipin kung paano tayo nagsimula. Isang random conversation lang dapat, then somehow, naging someone ka na lagi kong inaabangan kausap. From random topics, late night conversations, pang aasar, tampuhan, hanggang sa mga bagay na hindi naman natin planong pagusapan pero somehow naging part na ng pang araw-araw natin.

And habang tumatagal, mas lalo kitang nakikilala, not just the funny, makulit, and sometimes very mataray na version of you WAHAHAHAHAHA, but also the thoughtful, genuine, and soft side of you. I appreciate every little thing you share with me, kahit yung mga random thoughts mo na minsan mapapaisip talaga ako kung saan mo napulot. 😭

I made this website because I wanted to make something that feels a little more personal. Hindi lang siya basta website, gusto kong maging parang maliit na collection ng memories tas thoughts and syempre things that remind me of you. Something you can look back on and hopefully smile at.

I don't know exactly where this story will take us, and I don't want to rush it either. For now, I'm just happy that I met you Mai, happy that I get to know you little by little, and happy that somehow, out of all the random people we could've met, we ended up finding each other.

So ayun... please appreciate the effort ha. 😭 Pinaghirapan ko 'to, kaya bawal mang-asar nang sobra.

And if you ever wonder why I keep choosing to stay and know you more, I think the answer is pretty simple lang because you're you, and somehow, that's already enough reason for me to keep choosing you Mai 🤍

Always choosing you,
With all my heart❤️
